# Danlord's 3-Minute Live Virtual Pitch Script
**Event:** 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge — Iligan City Virtual Hub  
**Team:** Pew Pew  
**Project:** Mission Design Game  
**Target Duration:** 2 minutes 50 seconds (~400 words)

---

### [0:00 - 0:20] The Hook: Spacecraft as Compromise
Every spacecraft ever flown is an engineering compromise. 

If you add an extra camera, you need more electrical power. More power demands larger solar arrays. Bigger arrays demand a heavier rocket. And a heavier rocket quickly obliterates your mission budget.

### [0:20 - 0:35] The Problem & Educational Gap
Students and aspiring engineers rarely experience this reality. In textbooks and media, they see the fire of launch and the beauty of high-res images—not the grueling trade-offs made in systems engineering. So we built an interactive experience to let them feel those decisions firsthand.

### [0:35 - 1:05] The Solution: Pew Pew Mission Design Game
This is **Pew Pew: Mission Design Game**. 

Players step into the shoes of a NASA flight director across three authentic, flight-tested mission profiles: prospecting water ice at the Lunar South Pole with Artemis, hunting seasonal methane plumes on Mars, or rendezvous with the exposed metallic asteroid Psyche.

You configure launch vehicles, spacecraft buses, and flight-proven scientific instruments modeled directly on real NASA hardware.

### [1:05 - 1:40] The Gameplay & 6 Live Telemetry Gauges
As you build, our reactive telemetry HUD tracks the six essential resources live: Mass, Power, Budget, Data, Communications, and Time.

Add a high-resolution optical imager for Mars, and watch what happens: your communications telemetry surges past 100 percent. The spacecraft produces more daily data than the Deep Space Network downlink can handle—exactly the bottleneck real planetary orbiters face.

### [1:40 - 2:15] Scientific Validity & The Root-Cause Debrief
When you launch, realistic mid-mission events strike—from Coronal Mass Ejections to Deep Space Network scheduling locks.

And if your mission fails, we don’t just show a generic "Game Over." We provide a full **root-cause engineering debrief** featuring the player’s exact numbers: the Tsiolkovsky rocket equation, the inverse-square law for solar irradiance, and RF link-budget calculations. Students discover *why* their craft failed, internalize the physics, and redesign in seconds.

### [2:15 - 2:35] Technical Execution & Credibility
Is it scientifically valid? The equations are real orbital and physical mechanics. The instruments reflect flight-proven specs from LRO, MAVEN, ExoMars, and Psyche, with every dataset cited directly to NASA repositories. 

It is 100% free, runs in any modern browser on phones or laptops, requires no login, and is fully open-source.

### [2:35 - 3:00] Closing Call to Action
Our 6-person team from Iligan City built this entire platform over 48 hours to inspire the next generation of space explorers.

With Pew Pew: **Fail like an engineer. Learn like NASA.**

Thank you, and we look forward to your questions!
